Not every animated file needs to dominate the site.

## Controlled motion wins

Using motion in specific zones gives the site more polish than turning every section into an autoplay surface.

RAD's v1 approach keeps animation focused on:

- the hero
- the Discord/community callout
- selective branded support moments

## Why this works

This preserves performance, keeps the page readable, and leaves room for upgraded motion graphics later.
