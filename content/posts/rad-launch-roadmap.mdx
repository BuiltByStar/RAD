RAD is launching with a deliberate v1 structure instead of waiting for every premium asset to be finished.

## Why this matters

The right move for launch is to separate **site quality** from **media quality**.

That means:

- the information architecture is stable now
- roster and staff sections are already usable
- the content system is live from day one
- future brand upgrades can drop in later

## What changes later

When higher-end assets arrive, RAD can replace:

- logos
- wallpapers
- hero videos
- player or staff portraits
- sponsor marks

The site layout, routes, and content model stay intact.
